Copy for the landing page, video promotion and social posts.

---

**The problem**

### Deposits get mixed up. Totals get added by hand. Old invoices change.

When your bookings live in chats, spreadsheets and one-off invoices, small mistakes turn into big headaches. A deposit gets confused with full payment. An edit quietly changes what a client already received.

---

**Features**

### Book it in one form
Client, package, extra items, date and crew, captured together. The total updates as you type, so the price is always right before you confirm.

### Invoices that stay true
Issue a DP, a full payment, or follow-up payments. Every invoice is a permanent record of what was paid. Editing a booking later never rewrites history.

### Know what's due at a glance
Calendar, table and upcoming view show every booking, its status and its balance. Spot what needs attention today and this week before it becomes a problem.

### Professional invoices in one click
Download a clean A4 PDF with your logo, business details, payment instructions and terms. Update your business information once, and every future invoice carries it.

### Your package library, always current
Keep one list of packages and prices. Update a price once, and new bookings use it. Existing bookings keep the price they were agreed at.

### Find anything in seconds
Search by client, booking name or phone. Filter by status, crew, package or date. Share a filtered view by sending the link.

### Built for your team
Admins manage everything. Viewers can see bookings and invoices without changing them. Each crew member's schedule is one click away.

---

**How it works**

1. **Create the booking.** Add the client, package, extras and date. The total calculates itself.
2. **Take the payment.** Issue a DP or full invoice and it's recorded as paid right away.
3. **Track the rest.** Follow-up payments update the status automatically: Draft, Partially paid, Paid.
4. **Send the invoice.** Download the PDF and share it with your client.

---

**Trust strip**

- Every amount is exact, down to the Rupiah.
- Invoices never change after they're issued.
- Sessions close daily at midnight WIB, so access stays tight.
