# Pricing: Booking & Invoice Management

For small to mid-size organizers. Exchange rate: **1 USD = Rp 18.500**.

---

## 1. Operational cost (running the app)

| Service | USD / month | IDR / month | USD / year | IDR / year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Pro | $20 | Rp 370.000 | $200 | Rp 3.700.000 |
| Supabase Pro | $25 | Rp 462.500 | $300 | Rp 5.550.000 |
| Vercel Pro | $20 | Rp 370.000 | $240 | Rp 4.440.000 |
| **Total** | **$65** | **Rp 1.202.500** | **$740** | **Rp 13.690.000** |
| + Supabase server, per extra customer | $10 | Rp 185.000 | $120 | Rp 2.220.000 |

- The total is paid once, however many customers you have.
- The first customer's server is free (covered by Supabase's $10 credit). From the 2nd customer on, add Rp 185.000 per customer per month.
- Claude Pro is cheaper yearly ($200 instead of $240). Supabase and Vercel cost the same either way.
- Domain: free (`.vercel.app`).

## 4. Scenario: customer budget Rp 500.000 per month

Rp 500.000 can't cover the full operational cost (Rp 1.202.500 / month) on its own. But each extra customer only adds **Rp 185.000** (their server). So a small-budget customer is profitable **once another customer already covers the shared cost**.

## Suggestion

- **No minus from the first customer.** One customer pays for Claude, Supabase and Vercel. Every customer after that is mostly profit (about Rp 1.164.000 per month each on the 12-month plan).
- **Accounts are almost pure profit.** Supabase doesn't charge per user, so every crew or admin account you sell goes straight to profit.
- **The 3-month plan earns the most per month, and the 12-month plan earns the least.** But 12 months gives you a year of money up front, and the customer stays longer. Offer all three, and push 12 months to customers you want to keep.
- **The price is now higher.** Rp 1.199.000–1.399.000 per month is at the top of what small organizers usually pay. Once you have 3 or more customers, you can lower it and still never go minus.
- **Setup is free, done by you.** Use it as a selling point: "We set everything up for you."
- **Check Claude, Supabase and Vercel prices once before launch.** I took them from 2026 guides, not their official pages. If the Rupiah weakens a lot, check that the 12-month plan still covers the cost.
