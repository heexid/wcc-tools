## Load (`supabase/perf-seed.sql`, local / staging only)

On top of the normal seed:
- 42 packages (50 total)
- 10 000 bookings over 2025–2027, with 0–3 items and 1–3 crew each
- ~25 000 invoices following the invoice rules (DP or Full first, Partial(s), a final Paid; never above the total)

Everything goes through the real triggers, so totals, paid amounts and statuses are correct. Loads in about 10 s.

## What changed (migration 007, never deployed, edited in place)

1. **Rows built set-based.** `filter_bookings` (calendar, insights, upcoming, crew schedule) aggregated items, crew and invoice stats with about eight correlated subqueries **per booking**, each under RLS. It now uses one grouped aggregate per child table per query plus joins. Same JSON: read parity 76 / 76 compares it with the per-row `booking_row()`, which `get_booking` and single rows still use.
2. **Date bounds parsed once.** `try_date()` (an exception-block function) ran twice **per row**; now once per query.

Indexes were not changed. The month window filters ~10 k rows in the plan cheaply. The trigram indexes serve search through `search_booking_ids` (verified with EXPLAIN in P6). P11's "add or fix indexes" list (`booking_date`, `booking_crew(crew_id)`, `invoices(booking_id, issued_at desc)`, GIN trgm) has existed since P1.
