## Decisions / rules derived from Figma
- **Selected = anchor date.** Every Figma frame shows 12 Nov as *Selected* with no panel open, so Selected is the calendar's anchor (the day last clicked / arrowed to, the keyboard tab stop). Defaults to today (Today style wins). G3 loads `?date=2026-11-12`. Opening a popover doesn't move it (17 keeps 12 Selected).
- **Popover placement:** below the day, left-aligned with it, clamped 8 px (space/compact) inside the card's outer edge → 14 Nov lands exactly at Figma's (663, 974); from week 4 down it opens above.
- **Keyboard:** ← → ↑ ↓, Home / End, PageUp / PageDown, Enter / Space (popover or New booking). Chips / "+N more" / the hover target are pointer-only (tabindex −1); keyboard users reach every booking through the day popover.
- Figma's ghost-button "+" in the popover footer is overridden to text/primary (slip, as in 36) → DS text/accent, masked.
